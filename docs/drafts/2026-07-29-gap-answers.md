## 1. What timer setup actually works for ADHD?
`d2bc80f6-76d0-4be4-a613-e52a6e219626` · Tools & apps
> Pomodoro, flowmodoro, visual timers — what has actually stuck for you and why?

**Draft:**

Honestly, the thing that stuck for me isn't a timer I start — it's a sound that
happens whether or not I remembered to press anything. In our Check-ins room a
chime goes off at :00 and :30 (ascending at the top of the hour, descending at
the bottom), so I hear where I am in the hour without looking at a clock.

That's the part that always broke Pomodoro for me. I'd either forget to start
it, or I'd be deep in something good and resent the buzzer. A rhythm the room
keeps for me beats a timer I have to run myself.

## 2. What do you do when you lose focus mid-session?
`64a26792-1697-4483-aba9-ff3d304bd809` · Focus techniques
> How do you get back on track without spiraling or giving up for the day?

**Draft:**

The drift isn't what ends my day — the spiral about the drift is. So the thing
that helps most is just saying it out loud on the call: "I've been off in the
weeds for ten minutes." Naming it takes almost all the air out of it.

Then I pick the smallest possible way back in. Not "finish the thing" — just
open the file. Getting back to the desk counts as getting back on track.

## 3. Cleaning and organizing habits/systems that stick?
`e5445e29-54c3-4b8a-9341-8b6815ee01b0` · Routines & systems
> What are some cleaning and organizing rules/systems that have been able to keep
> away the (worst) messes and feelings of overwhelm for you?
> (tag me if you ever want to bd cleaning together unmuted to help each other out
> when we get stuck)

**Draft:**

Nothing that depends on me being consistent has ever lasted. Two things that
have:

Open bins, no lids. If I can't see it, it doesn't exist — so anything behind a
lid or a cupboard door is functionally gone, and the pile just forms somewhere
else.

And a floor instead of a standard. On a bad day the win is "one surface," not
"the kitchen." Lowering the bar is what keeps me from bouncing off it entirely.

Also — yes to the unmuted cleaning session. Tag me, I'm in.
