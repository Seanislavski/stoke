📚 The library needs your eyes

Two things, and the second one's the ask.

First - Silas can now search the Q&A library from here.

Try /library task or /library distractions. Only you see the result, unless you add share:true to post it in the channel — which is the real use case: someone asks a question that's been answered before, and you drop the answer right there.

Second - the library is thin, and that's the part I need help with. I checked today: of the 24 things I tried (which a member might plausibly search for), it can answer 13.

Here's what it comes up empty on:

procrastination · motivation · medication · sleep · rewards · burnout · accountability · hyperfocus · planners · anxiety · exercise

Every one of those gets talked about here regularly. None of it is saved anywhere. It scrolls past and it's gone.

That's what capturing is for. When you see someone drop a genuinely good answer - the kind you'd want to link to next time it comes up:

Right-click the message → Apps → 📚 Capture for Stoke

You don't have to ask them anything. Silas DMs the author himself and asks how they'd like it handled - with credit, anonymously, or not at all. Nothing gets published unless they say yes. If they say no, that's the end of it, and they're never asked again.

Capture generously. It costs the author one button press, and a "maybe" they turn down is completely fine - better than a good answer nobody ever finds again.
