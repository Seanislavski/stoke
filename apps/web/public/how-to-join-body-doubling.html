How to Join the
Body Doubling Community
Body Doubling is now on Stoke — a reliable place to build a shared, lasting library of the tools and tips that help neurodivergent minds focus and get things done. Here's how to join in under a minute.
What you'll need: a phone or computer, and an email address. That's it — joining is free, and there's nothing to download.
1
Open the link
On your phone or computer, go to stoke.community/communities/bodydoublingcom. You'll land on the Body Doubling community page.
2
Tap “Join BodyDoubling.com”
On the community page you'll see an orange Join BodyDoubling.com button. Give it a tap.
3
Create your free account
Enter your email and a password to make your Stoke account. Already have one? Choose “I already have an account” and sign in instead. No email confirmation needed — you're in right away.
4
You're in!
That's it — you're now a member of the Body Doubling community and land right inside it. One account works across all of Stoke, so you never sign up twice.
✓

Welcome to Body Doubling on Stoke

You can now work alongside the community, post on the bulletin board, RSVP to events, and add to our growing knowledge base — practical, hard-won tools and tips for neurodivergent minds.

Once you're in

  • Add a photo and a short bio so others can say hello.
  • Browse the channels and events to see what's happening.
  • Share a tool or tip that helps you focus — and search what's worked for others.
  • Introduce yourself on the bulletin board — we'd love to meet you.
